Ship's Topics navigation

As built, 2026-11-04 · shaped 2026-11-02 and reshaped to the approved prototype the same day · Ship project · Goal: Ship opens on Topics, and you find work by topic: a sidebar of Topics, Projects and Issues (then each Folder's own), and the three pages share one pattern of tabs with counts.

Ship learns to browse by topic. It opens on a Topics page, gains a page for each topic, a Projects page and a new sidebar, and everything about topics draws from one read of every topic and what it lists, kept in the shared package; a page that draws a topic's conversations in Peek and what is new for you reads them beside it, for the topics it shows. Nothing new is written to the relay: topics and their lists are the ones "Topics group files" built. Look first at the Map's "after": the green read in the middle is the riskiest part, and everything Ship draws hangs off it.

As built, 2026-11-04
