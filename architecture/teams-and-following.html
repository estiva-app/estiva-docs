Teams and following

As built, 2026-11-11 · shaped 2026-11-02 · Ship project · Goal: you see what is said in the teams you are in and the places you follow, at the level you chose, and nothing else.

"Today" is cut from Estiva architecture (as built, 23 Oct 2026).

Every Folder becomes a team: the same group, now with members and followers, and three kinds of place: its chat, which Peek draws for the first time; its spaces, the topics it marks as lasting; and the other topics it leads. Every topic or space has one switch per person: Not following, Important or All. Joining a topic is choosing a level, and someone can add you to one, at Important; you can always leave. One shared rule, in the code every app already uses, works out what is "in what you follow", and the relay makes sure only you set your own level. Start with the Map, then Objects for what a team is.

As built, the shape held, and the project grew by three issues the shaping did not draw. The relay says why it refuses: a refusal carries a short code, and Peek, Ship, Leaf and Claude each say a refused move in their own words from it. A team's last owner may leave it, and a team with no owner is looked after by the workspace's owners and admins. Adding someone to a team in Peek opens the standard Add members window, offering only the company's people. The Company team also does more than the shaping drew: the relay keeps everyone in the company on it, nobody leaves it, and the relay refuses a key that only does a job joining any team or being added to one. Where the code differs from the plan, the "after" pictures now draw what runs; "Today" is kept as the history.

Access

Teams are open in this stage, so for an open team "whoever can read the team" is everyone in the company; what a private team hides is not designed yet, so this table describes open teams. That is the intended default of this stage, not something left behind: private and secret teams are the next stage (SPEC §3.1; the draft's Not in scope).
