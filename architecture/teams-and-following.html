Teams and following

Shaped, 2 Nov 2026 · project draft, not yet in Ship · Goal: you see what is said in the teams you are in and the places you follow, at the level you chose, and nothing else.

"Today" is cut from Estiva architecture (as built, 23 Oct 2026).

Every Folder becomes a team: the same group, now with members and followers, and three kinds of place: its chat, which Peek draws for the first time; its spaces, the topics it marks as lasting; and the other topics it leads. Every topic or space has one switch per person: Not following, Important or All. Joining a topic is choosing a level, and someone can add you to one, at Important; you can always leave. One shared rule, in the code every app already uses, works out what is "in what you follow", and the relay makes sure only you set your own level. Start with the Map, then Objects for what a team is.

  • M1 The prototype shows a team's three kinds of place, its people, the switch on a place, Add, and who follows a place.
  • M2 The SPEC says what a team, its chat, its spaces, Add and following are; the relay keeps each person's level theirs alone; the shared code works out what you follow.
  • M3 Peek's Teams: each team's chat, spaces and topics, its people, Join, Leave, Follow and marking a space; following, joining or leaving a topic or space, adding someone, and who follows it.
  • M4 The Company team, with everyone in it; Ship, Leaf and Claude say Team.

Access

Every Folder is open today, so "whoever can read the team" is everyone in the company until private teams exist. That is the intended default of this stage, not something left behind: private and secret teams are the next stage (SPEC §3.1; the draft's Not in scope).
