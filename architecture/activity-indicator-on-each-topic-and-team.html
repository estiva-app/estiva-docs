Activity indicator on each topic and team

As built, 2026-11-16 · shaped 2026-11-10 · Ship project · Goal: in Peek, the dot on a topic, a space, a team's chat and a DM says why it is lit when you hover it, turns orange when someone reacts to your message there, and each keeps an Activity list you can open, by one rule every app can draw.

"Today" is cut from Estiva architecture (updated 2026-11-10 for Topics: Main and All); Estiva ID and the agent are left out, since nothing here touches them.

Two small things change on the relay. A reaction names the person whose message it reacts to, so any app can ask for the reactions naming you; and your own read state gains one "seen" mark per conversation for its orange, so a reaction is seen once, everywhere. Comments and mentions are already there. What changes most is the shared rule that decides whether a place's dot lights: it learns to say why (who, what, on which file), it reads the reactions naming you and lights them orange, and at Important it lights for what names you on a file the topic lists. Peek draws it: a blue or orange dot on a topic, a space, a team's chat and a DM, a hover that lists what lit it, and an Activity list for each place that keeps what the hover showed. Ship and Leaf draw no dot yet; they can later, by the same rule. Look first at the Flow.

As built, the shape held, and the prototype settled what the shaping left open. The Activity list is the dot beside a page's title: on a topic's, a space's, a team's, a team chat's or a DM's page, resting on it or tapping it lists New, everything that lights the place now, then Earlier, what was about you and is seen: a mention, a reply in your conversation, a reaction to your message. A team's page lists its chat, spaces and topics together. Both at once is one blue dot, and the card lists the reaction in orange. Resting on a row opens a card of who did what, where and when, in Topics, Teams, People and on the Desk. A topic's card lists every new comment on a file it lists, five and then "N more". What names you on a file one level under a listed file also lights a topic at Important: an issue of a listed project, a page under a listed document. "Seen" is written when the card or the title list closes, and also when you open the conversation that shows the reaction (decided 13 Nov). It is kept under a reactions: key per conversation, with a floor for keys a full slot drops. Every app names the author on a reaction: Peek, Ship, Leaf, and Buzz's own apps, agents and CLI. A reaction no longer wakes an agent as a mention. The follow switch's words say what each level shows and what lights it. The Map's and the Flow's "After" and the Objects are redrawn to match.
