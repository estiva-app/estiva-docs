Activity indicator on each topic and team

Shaped, 2026-11-10 · Ship project · Goal: in Peek, the dot on a topic, a space, a team's chat and a DM says why it is lit when you hover it, turns orange when someone reacts to your message there, and each keeps an Activity list you can open, by one rule every app can draw.

"Today" is cut from Estiva architecture (updated 2026-11-10 for Topics: Main and All); Estiva ID and the agent are left out, since nothing here touches them.

Two small things change on the relay. A reaction names the person whose message it reacts to, so any app can ask for the reactions naming you; and your own read state gains one "seen" mark per conversation for its orange, so a reaction is seen once, everywhere. Comments and mentions are already there. What changes most is the shared rule that decides whether a place's dot lights: it learns to say why (who, what, on which file), it reads the reactions naming you and lights them orange, and at Important it lights for what names you on a file the topic lists. Peek draws it: a blue or orange dot on a topic, a space, a team's chat and a DM, a hover that lists what lit it, and an Activity list for each place that keeps what the hover showed. Ship and Leaf draw no dot yet; they can later, by the same rule. Look first at the Flow.
