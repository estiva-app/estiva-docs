One conversation in every app

As built, 2026-11-09 · shaped 2026-10-28 · Ship project · Goal: a conversation looks and works the same in Peek, Ship and Leaf, as one shared piece each app places and colours.

Before this project each app drew its own comment cards, threads and menus around the shared comment box ("Today", kept as the history). As built, the conversation is one shared piece in the UI kit: each app wraps the kit's cards in a small adapter for its own words and data, says where the conversation sits, and colours it. Ship joined the apps that resolve a thread, and the relay gained one rule: a reply stays in its comment's team. Start with the Map.

The same events on the wire: a comment, a reply, an edit, a delete, a reaction and a resolve (SPEC §6.4, §6.6; KINDS.md). A Highlight is not one of them: it has no shape on the relay, and this project did not give it one (Highlights on the relay). What changed is that every app reads them into one shape and draws them one way, and two rules are now written down. SPEC has its own section, "Resolving a thread": anyone who may comment on the file may resolve or reopen, the newest counts, and a resolve counts only when it is sent to its comment's team. And a reply belongs to its comment's team: the relay refuses one whose comment is missing, deleted, unreadable to its writer, or in another team.
