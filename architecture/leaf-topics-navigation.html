Leaf's Topics navigation

As built, 2026-11-04 · shaped the same day · Goal: Leaf's sidebar shows your topics, Home, then Topics, then the teams, and a topic opens as its own page in Leaf

Leaf learns to browse by topic, as Ship did on 4 Nov. Its sidebar gets a Topics section between Home and the Folders tree, and each topic gets its own page in Leaf. Both draw from the one read of every topic that Ship and Add to topic already share. Nothing new is written to the relay.

There is no Objects, Flow or Access view. No object changes what it is. Who can read or change a topic or its list stays as "Topics group files" left it. The only move between apps is the one link from a topic's page to its conversation in Peek.
