Each company sets its own statuses

Shaped, 2026-10-30 · Goal: a project's or an issue's status says what it means on the wire, so every app counts progress the same way without looking anything up in Ship.

Today a status change says only which status, such as In Progress. To count progress, Peek and Leaf look up what that status means in Ship's listing, and guess from its words when they cannot. After this project every status change also carries its stage (open, started, done or dropped), so any app counts from the change itself. The SPEC also says where a company's own statuses will live and which fields search reads. Nothing writes that company file yet, and no screen changes. Start with the Objects view.
