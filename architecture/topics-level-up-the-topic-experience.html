Topics: level up the topic experience

Shaped, 2026-11-06 · Goal: your topics are easy to find and arrange in Peek, Ship and Leaf. They come in the order things happen, any topic (archived ones too) is one click away, and a topic's files sit the way the team arranged them.

Two things change under the screens. First, every app orders your topics by one shared rule, the newest thing anyone did in the topic, worked out from what is already on the relay. Second, a topic's list of files gains an order and a look per file, kept on the topic and the same for everyone. Everything else is how each app draws topics: Peek flat, Ship and Leaf five topics folded, and Explore topics in all three.
