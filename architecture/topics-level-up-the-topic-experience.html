Topics: level up the topic experience

As built, 2026-11-11 · shaped 2026-11-06 · Ship project · Goal: your topics are easy to find and arrange in Peek, Ship and Leaf. They come in the order things happen, any topic (archived ones too) is one click away, and a topic's files sit the way the team arranged them.

Two things change under the screens. First, every app orders your topics by one shared rule, where anything last moved: the newest message in the topic's conversation, a file added to it, or a change or comment on a file it lists (issues under a listed project too, the agent's writes included). Arranging, row or card, a rename and joining do not count. The order is worked out from what is already on the relay. Second, a topic's list of files gains an order and a look per file, kept on the topic and the same for everyone. Everything else is how each app draws topics: Peek flat, Ship and Leaf five topics folded, and Explore topics in all three.

As built, both held, with one addition and one sharpening. Added: Leaf's topic page carries the topic's conversation beside it, as Ship's does, in place of the link to Peek. A reply from Leaf is an ordinary message, and reading there counts as read in Peek and Ship (a ninth issue, 11 Nov). Sharpened: each sidebar fold lists only what its app opens. Ship's lists projects and issues. Leaf's lists pages, and projects and issues whose text it can show. Archived files stay out of both, so a topic whose files are all archived folds to nothing. A comment on an issue under a listed project still moves the topic, as the rule says (milestone 6, decided 10 Nov). The Map's "After" and the Flow are redrawn to match.
