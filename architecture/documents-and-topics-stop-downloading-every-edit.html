Documents and topics stop downloading every edit

Shaped, 2026-11-19 · Ship project · Last stage of "One current version of every file", after projects and issues · Goal: all four file types follow one rule, with no way left to read or write a file the old way.

Projects and issues are read as they are now and written in order. This stage moves the last two types, Leaf documents and Peek topics, at one announced moment, and then removes the old way from the relay, the apps and the agent. Look first at the Map: documents are where the load is (12.3 MB of old saves), topics are where the writes are widest (all three apps add files to a topic). Then the Flow: the switch can be turned back until Miky and Katerina have each looked, and not after.
