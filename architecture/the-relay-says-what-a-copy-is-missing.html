The relay says what a copy is missing

Shaped, 2026-11-23 · Ship project · Goal: when Miky reopens Peek, the Desk is up to date within seconds and downloads only what changed.

Peek draws its Desk from the copy on the device at once, then asks the relay for everything again: as Miky, about 170 questions, 40 MB and 30 seconds on every reopen. This project teaches the relay to say what a copy is missing (Nostr's set reconciliation, NIP-77), and has Peek ask for the Desk's own rows first. Look at the Flow first: it is the reopen, before and after.

  • A reopen as Miky. Production, 23 Nov, three reopens of /app/desk from the copy: Desk text at 25–32 ms, 165–178 questions, 40–41 MB (socket and HTTP), quiet at about 30.5 s; kind 1851 (file edits) about 25 MB. A cold open: 148 questions, 43 MB, quiet at 21 s. Kit: catchup-capture.js with the probe, signed in with Miky's delegated session.
  • The relay has no NIP-77. Buzz origin/nfb-demo-kinds: no NEG- handling; an unknown verb gets a NOTICE (crates/buzz-relay/src/protocol.rs:227).
  • Who can read, three places. handlers/req.rs:57 (questions), handlers/count.rs:236 (counts), api/bridge.rs:1469 (HTTP), part in the database and part per event afterwards (req.rs:556-578).
  • 1,000 per question. crates/buzz-db/src/store/event.rs:33; a question with no limit gets 1,000 (req.rs:1204). The decision record says 500, which is what Block's apps ask for.
  • 50 requests per 5 s, per person across connections, counting questions, counts and writes (rejection.rs:49).
  • The copy. packages/platform/src/event-snapshot.ts in estiva-foundation: per question, held events the answer leaves out are dropped (:435-493); 10,000 events. Peek only: Ship and Leaf do not use it, and no shared page exists in any repo.
  • Not verified: when the Desk's own rows are current on a reopen (milestone 1 measures it); how many events the Desk's questions match in all, which decides how large a catch-up the relay must allow.
