What a team is

When a team gets members, followers and its own chat, is it today's Folder (the same place that holds the files and decides who can read them), or a new thing above it? And is it called Team or Folder?

This replaces the brief "Team or Folder", which asked only for the word and took it as given that a team is today's Folder. Nothing written down decides that, and the answer decides the shape.

Today

A Folder is where a file lives. It lists its files, and the relay lets you read a file only if you can read its Folder. Production has 9 Folders: Estiva HQ, Peek, Ship, Leaf, Steer, QA, Backlog, Folders and Test folder Katerina. All 9 are open to everyone in the company, and each lists one person as a member. Each Folder has its own chat on the relay, but no app shows it.

Since 28 Oct you have talked about teams:

  • "Teams represent how the company is structured… Teams is where the file lives. There is default team – whole company" (Leaf "Navigation", 28 Oct).
  • "The team decides who can see what": a secret team shows nothing outside it, and a private team chooses which of its things are public. "A team holds a private group and a public group… People see one team, never the groups" (29 Oct).
  • "The file still lives in Folder (in future we will rename to Team). Topic is group of files across different teams" (30 Oct).

Those are Decided lines on the Navigation project, which you called "too soon for estiva-docs". The records say something different. The Accepted record of 25 Sep says "It is called a Folder; team is not a second name for it". The Company Folder record of 30 Oct leaves "there is always a Company team" to "that rework decides it". The record "Folders are labels" (access is decided on the file) is still Proposed, although your 29 Oct answer went the other way.

Why it reaches beyond this stage

This is the first thing any app, or a builder at an outside company, has to understand: where a file lives, who can read it, and who is told about it. If these are the same object, a builder learns one thing. If they are two objects, they learn two, and they have to keep the two in step.

It also decides how the private stage is built. You want a team to have a private part and a public part. Option C puts a place for both on the relay now. A and B leave that for the private stage.

A and B can be undone. C cannot. Every file records its Folder's id, and that id never changes. Under A or B, a separate team object could still be added later and pointed at the existing Folder as its public part, with no file moving. C adds a new kind of object to the relay that every app reads, and writes one for each of the 9 Folders, and those stay on the relay. The word itself is not on the relay. The one exception is the label every app reads for a Folder ("folder"), which changes in a single package release.

Options

A · A team is today's Folder, and it is called Team recommended

  • What you and Katerina see. Peek's rail says Teams. Katerina presses Join on Peek, and from then on a new conversation in Peek's chat lights for her. Ship's Move control, Leaf's page header, Claude and the CLI all say Team on the same day.
  • What an outside company gets. One object. A team is where a file lives, who can read it, and who is told. Notion's teamspaces and Linear's teams work the same way.
  • What it locks in. Very little. A file lives in one team, as it lives in one Folder today. A file two teams care about goes in a topic, which can hold files from any team ("Topic is group of files across different teams").
  • What could go wrong.
    • Some of the 9 are not groups of people: "Folders", "Test folder Katerina", "Backlog" and "QA" become teams with those names until you rename or archive them. Who is in each team on day one comes in the product questions.
    • The 25 Sep record's lesson works in reverse: old text says Folder, and sessions will repeat it for a while.
    • When the private stage gives a team a second, private part, it might find this shape awkward and want C. C can still be added then, because nothing moves.

B · A Folder gains people and stays a Folder do less

This is the same object as A, and the word Folder stays. The 25 Sep record stands unchanged, and nothing is renamed in any app, in the SPEC or in Claude.

C · A team is a new thing, and today's Folder becomes its public group

  • What you and Katerina see. The same screens as A. The groups are never shown.
  • What an outside company gets. Two objects, a team and its groups, and a rule for keeping them in step. A secret team and a private team have the same shape from the first day.
  • What it locks in. A new kind of object on the relay, read by every app before it can draw a team. One is written for each of the 9 Folders, and the Company Folder's mark moves to a team or is kept twice.
  • What could go wrong. The relay checks reads, writes and who is told against groups, not against the team. The team object therefore adds a name and a pointer, plus a second copy of the members, which can disagree with the roster the relay actually enforces. And it settles the two groups now, which the private stage was meant to decide with private teams in front of it.

Not offered: teams beside the Folders, with a Teams list of people and a Folders list of files on screen side by side. Your 28 Oct "Teams is where the file lives" and your 29 Oct "people see one team" both rule it out.

How others do it

  • Notion teamspaces: pages live in a teamspace, which has members and is open, closed or private, and you join or leave it. This is A. What is different for us is that our followers see a team's public spaces without joining it.
  • Linear teams: an issue belongs to one team, a team can be private, and you join it. This is A. Linear has no followers of a team.
  • Slack: a channel is both the place and the access. User groups (@eng) are a separate list of people with no files, and admins keep the two in step by hand. That is the "beside" shape, and keeping it in step is the cost.
  • Upstream NIP-29: a group is its people, its chat and who may write. We already add a list of files to it. A keeps one group as one team; C adds an object NIP-29 does not have.

Recommendation

A: a team is today's Folder, and it is called Team everywhere. One object answers where a file lives, who can read it and who is told, as you described on 28 Oct ("Teams is where the file lives"). An outside company learns one thing, and it works the way Notion's and Linear's teams do. A does not decide the two groups. It leaves that to the private stage, where private teams will be in front of us, and C can still be added then without moving a file. B gives the same object a word you have already moved away from. C builds the private stage's answer now, with a second copy of the members that can disagree with what the relay enforces.

If you choose A, two Accepted records change: "It is called a Folder" is superseded by one that says Team is the only word, and the Company Folder becomes the Company team, with the same mark. The Proposed record "Folders are labels" closes as Rejected, citing your 29 Oct "the team decides". CLI flags, tool names and links keep "folder" for now.
