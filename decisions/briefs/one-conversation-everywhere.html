Decision brief · Peek moves onto the shared box · 20 October 2026

How much of a conversation is the same in every app?

Should a conversation (its comments, replies, menus, notes and layout) be one shared thing that looks and behaves the same in Peek, Ship and Leaf, with only each app's colours its own?

Today

Since 20 October Leaf and Ship write and react with one shared comment box and one shared reaction row, and Peek moves onto them next. Everything around the box is still each app's own: the comment card, the thread and its replies, the Edit/Delete menu, "This comment was deleted.", "(edited)", how a link alone on its line draws, and the words. You decided this on 16 October (record every-app-writes-and-reacts-with-one-shared-box: "the cards stay each app's own"). It was on Katerina's 22 September ruling that the cards Ship and Peek both draw are recorded and "none promoted yet", and her 23 September ruling that "(edited)" stays Peek's.

Today you said: "the capabilities should be shared and work the same including the layout. But the styling, the colors, should be on the app… so people don't need to have multiple mental models of conversations based on where they are."

What already differs between the apps because each draws its own cards (from the 20 October triage):

  • In Ship, a thread whose comment was deleted shows no "This comment was deleted." and still takes replies; Peek and Leaf close it.
  • Leaf's "(edited)" has no time on hover; Peek's does.
  • A link alone on its line is a row in Peek and a chip in Leaf.
  • A refused reaction's note stays until your next click in Leaf and goes by itself in Ship.
  • A file that is too large reads "Too large" in Leaf and "upload failed: 413 …" in Ship.
  • Ship shows no quoted words for a comment on part of a page.

Why it reaches beyond this ticket

This sets what an app, an outside company's included, takes from us to have comments: a box it wires up next to cards it draws itself, or a whole conversation it places and colours. It decides whether Katerina designs a conversation once or three times, and whether every fix to how a thread behaves is one release or three. Two-way door: nothing on the relay changes in any option, since all three write the same events. Undoing it means moving apps back onto cards of their own.

Options

A · Keep today's record. You and Katerina keep meeting the differences above. Each one is a ticket in each app. An outside company wires our box and reaction row into cards it builds itself. Katerina's 22 September ruling stands as written.

B · One conversation, each app's colours. A comment, a thread, a deleted comment, an edit, a link alone on its line and a refusal read and behave the same in Peek, Ship and Leaf, in the same layout. Peek's thread is dark Signal, Ship's is Ship's blue, Leaf's is Leaf's. Where it sits stays each app's: a pane in Peek, Activity in Ship, a column beside the blocks in Leaf, and the shared layout has to fit Leaf's narrow column. Katerina designs the one conversation as a prototype before it is built, and her "promote none yet" for the conversation card ends there. An outside company places one component and gets all of it. It is the drop-in conversation the 16 October record turned down, keeping what that record protected: each app's own look, now its colours, not its layout.

C · One set of rules, three layouts. Behaviour and words are shared; each app draws its cards. You meet the same behaviour in a different layout in each app. An outside company still builds the drawing. A bug in drawing (a missing note, a chip where a row belongs) can still differ by app.

How others do it

  • Google Workspace: Docs, Sheets and Slides share one comment thread, card and menu, placed beside each app's content. This is option B.
  • Atlassian: Jira and Confluence comments are one shared component under each product's theme.
  • Matrix: each client draws its own timeline from shared rules (option C), and threads and edits behave differently across clients. That is the drift an open protocol gets without a reference component.

Recommendation

B: one conversation, each app places it and colours it. A comment is the same object everywhere (your 16 October words), and B makes it look and act that way too: a person learns one conversation, and an outside company adopting the protocol gets the reference reading of its comment rules instead of writing its own. It keeps each app's identity where Katerina put it, in its theme's colours. It is a stage of its own: Katerina's prototype first, then the build. This project, Peek onto the shared box, stays as shaped and comes first, so Peek is on the shared box before the conversation around it is shared.

One part of B to settle with it: does "capabilities are shared" include Peek's Resolve and Highlights, so Ship and Leaf offer them too? The 16 October project kept them Peek's ("each app offers only the rows it can perform"). They write Peek's own markers, which Ship and Leaf would then read and draw, so it is its own question, asked after this one.
