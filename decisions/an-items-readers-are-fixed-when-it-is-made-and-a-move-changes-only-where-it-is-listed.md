## Who it affects

Miky's own example: he marks Katerina's message in a Peek topic, and an
Insight appears on a Ship project. When the people who read that topic are
the people who read the project, nothing here stops it. When they differ, as
with a private team, a person's note or a direct message, the Insight could
show her words to people who were never in that conversation. That case
waits for the brainstorm on private teams and notes, as Miky asked on
13 November for a private DM.

## Context

- A file created under another file is stored with it, so only that file's
  readers read it
  ([what-belongs-to-a-file-is-stored-with-it](what-belongs-to-a-file-is-stored-with-it.md)).
  An item is a file made under its home.
- A listing never grants, widens or narrows access; a move changes only where a
  file is listed
  ([moving-a-file-never-changes-who-can-read-it](moving-a-file-never-changes-who-can-read-it.md)).
- The home record let an item's home change and said an item is read by its
  home's readers, which after a move would mean its readers change with it.
- Two records of 13 November left undecided only a highlight from a private
  conversation into a project the whole team reads. An item names its sources,
  and its title, summary and details may repeat their words.

## Decision

- **An item's readers are fixed when it is made**, as every file's are: they
  are the readers of the home it is made in.
- **Moving its home changes only where it is listed.** Those who read the new
  home but not the item do not see it there, as with any file they cannot read.
- **Not decided here, and not built before it is:** an item whose source is
  read by other people than its home (a private DM, a private team, a note, or
  any conversation or page with other readers), and whether an item copies its
  source's words. Both are brainstormed again with private teams and notes.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A moved item is read by its new home's readers** (the home record as
  merged). Turned down: it contradicts the two records above; a file's readers
  are set where it is stored, and no move changes them.
- **Leave undecided only a private DM.** Turned down: a private team or a note
  carries the same risk, and an item that repeats its source's words carries
  them to whoever reads the item.

## Consequences

- Moving an item is safe: it never shows the item to anyone new, and never
  hides it from anyone who could read it.
- An item moved to a home with more readers stays out of view for the new
  ones; how an app shows that, or lets someone share it wider, is shaped with
  the build.
- Marking a source whose readers differ from the home's waits for the private
  teams and notes brainstorm.
