# Intelligence works for you in the background and never takes your attention: what it finds lands in its home, and it reaches you only the way a person's message does, by naming you

- Date: 2026-11-13
- Status: Accepted
- Decided by: Miky, in the brainstorm session of 13 November 2026: "Intelligence is working for you async and it doesn't try to steal your attention"; what it finds lands in its home, and only a message that mentions you reaches your Desk; Ship Home shows which projects have new activity; and, in his comments that day on the Leaf page "Highlights and the intelligence layer", using the Activity indicator's rules for it, "not a bad idea", and an agent follows the same rules as a person; written up as the brief "What a project knows" ([page](briefs/highlights-and-what-a-project-knows.html))
- Area: Read state

## Who it affects

Ship's agent finds three insights and a risk in a week of Peek messages about a
project. Miky gets no notification for them. They wait, Proposed, in the
project's tabs. On Ship Home the project shows that something is new, the way a
topic does, and he looks when he chooses to. Only if the agent writes a message
that mentions him does it reach his Desk, exactly as Katerina's would.

## Context

- Intelligence here means what apps and agents suggest: Insights, Journal
  entries and Risks at Proposed
  ([what-a-project-learns-decides-and-worries-about-are-insights-journal-entries-and-risks](what-a-project-learns-decides-and-worries-about-are-insights-journal-entries-and-risks.md)),
  in the item's home
  ([an-insight-journal-entry-or-risk-lives-on-the-most-specific-place-it-fits](an-insight-journal-entry-or-risk-lives-on-the-most-specific-place-it-fits.md)).
- What reaches you is decided by what you follow and at which level, and at
  Important only what names you lights a place
  ([a-team-has-members-and-followers-and-a-place-is-followed-at-a-level](a-team-has-members-and-followers-and-a-place-is-followed-at-a-level.md),
  [at-important-what-names-you-on-a-listed-file-lights-the-topic](at-important-what-names-you-on-a-listed-file-lights-the-topic.md)).
- The Activity indicator being built in Peek draws blue for something to read
  and orange for something to know, with no counts.
- Loops, intelligence that runs on its own in the background, are not in the
  first version; Miky waits on decisions about them. This record holds for
  them when they come.

## Decision

- **Intelligence never takes your attention.** What an app or agent finds
  lands in its home, and you see it when you go there.
- **It reaches you only the way a person's message does**: by naming you, under
  the same rules of following and levels. An agent gets no other way in.
- **Ship Home's list of projects shows which have something new**, with the
  Activity indicator's rules.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A separate intelligence inbox or notification stream**, listing every
  suggestion as it comes. Turned down: it competes with people for attention
  and grows with every app and agent added, which is what today's SaaS AI
  does; Miky's call is the opposite.

## Consequences

- Adding an agent or an app's intelligence never adds notifications. Only its
  mentions can, and those follow the person's own levels.
- A Proposed item nobody looks at stays where it is; how a tab or a stale Risk
  invites review, without a notification, is shaped with the build.
- When an agent may mention someone, and how often, is open, with loops.
