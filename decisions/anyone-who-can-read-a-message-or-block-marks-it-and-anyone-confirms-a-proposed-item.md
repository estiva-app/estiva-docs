# Anyone who can read a message or a block can mark it or unmark it, other people's included, and anyone can confirm a Proposed item; an agent never confirms its own suggestion

- Date: 2026-11-13
- Status: Accepted
- Decided by: Miky, in the brainstorm session of 13 November 2026: anyone can mark or unmark any message, other people's included ("Katerina is shy to mark it, I will do it for her"), and anyone can confirm, a "flexible system"; and, by merging this record, that an agent never confirms its own suggestion, which was Claude's addition on the Leaf page "Highlights and the intelligence layer" that he did not object to; written up as the brief "What a project knows" ([page](briefs/highlights-and-what-a-project-knows.html)), where its Checked names that addition
- Area: Messages

## Who it affects

Miky reads Katerina's message in Peek and thinks it is a real insight. She is
shy to mark it, so he marks it for her. Later, either of them sees a Proposed
Journal entry that Ship's agent drafted from a thread and confirms it, and it
is gone from the other's triage too.

An agent may suggest as much as it likes, but what it suggests stays Proposed
until a person confirms it.

## Context

- An item is an Insight, a Journal entry or a Risk, made by marking a message
  or block, or suggested at Proposed by an app or an agent
  ([what-a-project-learns-decides-and-worries-about-are-insights-journal-entries-and-risks](what-a-project-learns-decides-and-worries-about-are-insights-journal-entries-and-risks.md)).
- Anyone who may comment on a file resolves or reopens its threads, and the
  newest counts
  ([anyone-who-may-comment-resolves-or-reopens-a-thread-and-the-newest-counts](anyone-who-may-comment-resolves-or-reopens-a-thread-and-the-newest-counts.md)).
- A file keeps every change for good, with a History panel
  ([a-file-keeps-every-change-for-good](a-file-keeps-every-change-for-good.md)).
- An agent signs for an app only by an admin's grant
  ([an-agent-signs-for-an-app-only-by-an-admin-grant](an-agent-signs-for-an-app-only-by-an-admin-grant.md)),
  and its profile says whose agent it is
  ([an-agents-profile-says-whose-it-is](an-agents-profile-says-whose-it-is.md)).

## Decision

- **Anyone who can read a message or a block can mark it** as a highlight, or
  unmark it, whoever wrote it.
- **Anyone who may comment on a Proposed item can confirm it**, or reject it,
  as anyone who may comment resolves a thread. Triage is the team's job: once
  one person has confirmed an item, it is confirmed for everyone.
- **An agent never confirms its own suggestion.** What an agent proposes waits
  for a person.
- Every mark, unmark, confirm and change of an item is kept in its history, so
  it shows who did what, and the apps' intelligence learns from it.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Only the author marks their own message or block.** Turned down: the
  person who said the insightful thing is often not the one who sees it is
  one, and Katerina's insight would stay unmarked.
- **Only the person who decided confirms a Journal entry.** Turned down: it
  stalls whenever that person is away, and the team, not one person, keeps the
  project's record; the item's Decided by already says who decided.

## Consequences

- A mark on someone else's words is visible to everyone who reads the item, and
  its history says who made it.
- An agent's work is always reviewed by a person before it counts, however many
  agents and apps suggest.
- With no person reviewing, Proposed items pile up; how a tab keeps that
  readable is shaped with the build.
- Marking a message from a private conversation into a project is not decided
  here; it is brainstormed with private teams and notes.
